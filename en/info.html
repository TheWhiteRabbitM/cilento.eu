By car, and when

A2 exits Battipaglia–Eboli for Paestum and Agropoli; Atena Lucana–Sala Consilina– Padula-Buonabitacolo for the interior; superstrada for Policastro and Sapri.

SS18 costiera: Agropoli → Palinuro are 55 km in ~1h20, slow and beautiful. In summer parking is full before 10.

Locally: summer Cilento Blu bus between the villages, shared boat at Palinuro/Camerota from €25. We book nothing: times and prices change, check at the harbor, museum, station.
